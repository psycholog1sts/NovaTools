General tool accuracy disclaimer

MC NovaTools provides browser-based utilities and written guides for practical preparation work. The tools can help merge, split, inspect, format, convert, calculate, compress, rename, compare, and organize information, but they cannot guarantee that every input is complete, every file is valid, every assumption is correct, or every output is suitable for a final decision. Results may vary by browser, device memory, file structure, encoding, locale, third-party library behavior, user settings, and the quality of the original source.

Always keep the original file or source data. Review page order, formatting, metadata, units, dates, filenames, special characters, rounding, and edge-case warnings before using an output. If a result affects money, taxes, legal obligations, compliance, accessibility, security, publishing, client delivery, or official records, obtain a qualified human review.

A tool may appear to complete successfully while still requiring review. For example, a PDF merge can preserve pages but not the business meaning of their order; an image compressor can reduce size but damage text legibility; a JSON formatter can make syntax readable without proving the API response is semantically correct; and a text converter can change characters in ways that matter for legal names, product codes, or URLs. Treat the output as prepared material, not automatic approval.

Finance and calculator disclaimers

Finance calculators on MC NovaTools are educational estimation tools. Mortgage, refinance, compound interest, retirement, student loan, tax, insurance, currency, crypto, stock, cloud cost, and similar calculators depend on assumptions you enter and on simplified formulas or reference data. They do not know your full financial situation, tax filing status, credit profile, lender terms, insurance underwriting, local law, transaction fees, market timing, inflation exposure, or risk tolerance.

No finance page on MC NovaTools is financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, legal advice, accounting advice, lending advice, or insurance advice. Do not make a purchase, sale, loan, refinance, tax filing, investment, insurance, or retirement decision solely from a tool result. Compare outputs with official documents and consult qualified professionals.

Finance examples are especially sensitive because small changes in assumptions can create large changes in conclusions. A mortgage refinance estimate can change with closing costs and time horizon; a retirement projection can change with inflation and contribution timing; a crypto tax estimate can change with jurisdiction, lot selection, and transaction history; and a cloud-cost estimate can change with usage spikes, discounts, and provider pricing updates. Results may vary, and the person using the result must verify the assumptions.

Browser processing and privacy limits

Many MC NovaTools workflows are designed to run locally in the browser. That privacy-first design reduces unnecessary file transfer, but it does not make every workflow risk-free. Your browser, extensions, operating system, downloads folder, clipboard, localStorage, cache, and network environment can still affect privacy. Avoid using shared computers for sensitive work unless you can clear data afterward.

Some pages may load third-party resources for advertising, analytics, fonts, embedded policies, live reference data, or external links. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices. The Privacy Policy explains Google AdSense, Google Analytics, cookies, and localStorage in more detail.

A link does not mean MC NovaTools controls or endorses every statement, product, or policy on that external site.
