Cookies & Browser Storage

Cookie Policy

This page explains how MC NovaTools may use cookies or similar browser technologies for essential site behavior, preferences, and limited operational functionality.

Last Updated: January 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar browser storage technologies that websites may use to remember information between visits, improve basic functionality, and support certain site features.

Our General Approach

MC NovaTools aims to keep data handling minimal and proportionate. We do not design the site around aggressive profiling or unnecessary tracking. Where cookies or similar technologies are used, the goal is to support practical site behavior rather than create heavy personal-data dependence.

Types of Storage That May Be Used

  • Preference storage: for example, remembering selected language or similar interface preferences
  • Essential functionality: browser-side state needed for normal page behavior
  • Operational or analytics-related technologies: limited tools that may help us understand performance, reliability, or high-level usage trends

What We Aim to Avoid

  • Unnecessary tracking-heavy architecture for ordinary tool use
  • Forced account-based personalization to access public tools
  • Overly intrusive storage unrelated to normal site operation

Third-Party Technologies

Some pages or services may rely on third-party infrastructure, analytics, or delivery layers. Those services may use their own technical mechanisms as part of normal site operation and request handling.

Managing Cookies and Browser Storage

You can typically review, restrict, or delete cookies and local browser storage from your browser settings. Depending on your choices, some parts of the site may behave differently or lose saved preferences.

Questions About Cookies

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how MC NovaTools handles browser-side technologies, you can contact the site owner directly.
