Cookie Policy

Minimal cookies, maximum privacy

Last Updated: January 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Our Cookie Philosophy

At MC NovaTools, we believe in minimal data collection. Unlike most websites that use numerous tracking cookies for advertising and analytics, we use cookies only when absolutely necessary for core functionality.

Cookies We Use

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They include:

  • Theme Preference: Remembers your dark/light mode preference
  • Language Selection: Stores your preferred language for future visits
  • Session State: Maintains tool state during your session

Duration: These cookies persist for up to 1 year or until you clear your browser data.

2. Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality:

  • Tool Preferences: Remembers your settings for specific tools
  • Accessibility Settings: Stores font size and contrast preferences

3. Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We use privacy-focused analytics that collect aggregate, non-identifiable data:

  • No personal identification
  • No cross-site tracking
  • Anonymized IP addresses

You can opt out of analytics cookies through your browser settings.

Cookies We Do NOT Use

We want to be clear about what we don't do:

  • No Advertising Cookies: We don't use cookies for targeted advertising
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We don't allow third-party tracking scripts
  • No Social Media Trackers: No Facebook, Twitter, or other social tracking
  • No Behavioral Profiling: We don't build profiles of your online activity

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

Cookie Consent

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy. When you first visit, you may see a cookie banner allowing you to customize your preferences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as needed. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
