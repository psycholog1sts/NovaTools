Security & Privacy

How we protect your data

Our Security Philosophy

At MC NovaTools, security isn't an afterthought—it's the foundation of everything we build.

Client-Side Processing

Unlike traditional online tools that require uploading files to remote servers, MC NovaTools processes everything locally in your browser:

  • Your files never leave your device
  • Zero upload to external servers
  • Zero storage on our servers
  • Zero access by our team

Technical Security Measures

  • HTTPS Encryption: All connections use TLS 1.3
  • Content Security Policy: Prevents XSS attacks
  • No Third-Party Scripts: Minimal external dependencies
  • Regular Security Audits: Automated vulnerability scanning
