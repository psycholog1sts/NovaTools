What Pro will not change

  • Existing useful Free tools will not be made intentionally worse just to force an upgrade.
  • NovaTools will not claim “unlimited” when browser memory, device capability or safety limits still apply.
  • File contents will not be moved into an account or billing control plane by default.
  • Cancellation and billing management must be self-service before live subscriptions are enabled.

Pricing questions

Can I buy Pro now?

No. Billing is not enabled yet. The interest buttons are a value-validation signal only and do not create a checkout, account or subscription.

Are these final prices?

No. $12 monthly and $120 annual are planned launch hypotheses. Final pricing may change before billing is enabled.

Will Free require an account?

The current product direction keeps core Free tools usable without forced signup.

Will Pro upload my files?

Planned Pro workflow features should preserve browser-first file processing wherever technically practical. Account and billing metadata are a separate server-side boundary.
