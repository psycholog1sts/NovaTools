Short bio

Metehan Çetin leads MC NovaTools product direction and public-content review. His work on the project focuses on browser-based utility workflows, clear privacy boundaries, accessibility-aware page structure, practical limitations, and user guidance that matches the implemented behavior.

Review areas

  • PDF workflows: merge, split, page-order review, metadata considerations, output naming and browser memory limits.
  • Image workflows: resizing, compression trade-offs, transparent backgrounds and export checks.
  • Developer utilities: JSON formatting, URL encoding, Base64 conversion, text diffs, static routing and structured data.
  • Privacy-first product language: local-processing boundaries, consent-aware analytics and safe support examples.

Review scope

Metehan reviews public pages for clear inputs, realistic limitations, safe handling of user files, accessibility, route stability, internal linking, and honest product claims. Calculator results are presented as informational estimates rather than professional financial advice, and browser tools are not described as substitutes for licensed legal, tax, investment, security, medical, or accounting review.

When a page involves money, compliance, publishing, security, or client delivery, the editorial standard is to include assumptions, verification steps, and an explicit limitation where professional review may be needed.
