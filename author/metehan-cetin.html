Short bio

Metehan Çetin, LPC leads MC NovaTools content and trust-page review with more than five years of hands-on experience building, testing, and explaining practical browser-based workflows. His work focuses on PDF preparation, image optimization, developer utilities, privacy-first product language, accessibility-aware page structure, and finance-calculator disclaimers that help users understand assumptions before relying on outputs.

Areas of knowledge

  • PDF Processing: merge, split, page-order review, metadata risk, output naming and browser memory limits.
  • Image Optimization: resizing, compression trade-offs, transparent backgrounds, email/social export checks and legibility review.
  • Web Development: JSON formatting, URL encoding, Base64 conversion, text diffs, static routing, structured data and Core Web Vitals.
  • Privacy-first workflows: localStorage boundaries, client-side processing language, consent-aware analytics and safe support examples.

Review scope

Metehan reviews public pages for clear inputs, realistic limitations, safe handling of user files, accessibility, route stability, internal linking, and SEO trust signals. He does not present calculator results as professional financial advice and does not claim that a browser tool can replace licensed legal, tax, investment, security, medical, or accounting review.

When an article or tool guide involves money, compliance, publishing, security, or client delivery, his editorial standard is to include assumptions, verification steps, and a clear recommendation to consult a qualified professional when the decision is significant.
