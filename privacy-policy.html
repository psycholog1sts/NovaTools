Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our foundation

Last Updated: January 2026

Introduction

MC NovaTools is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and online tools. Our approach is simple: we don't collect your data.

Our Core Privacy Principle

Unlike most online services that require you to upload files to their servers, MC NovaTools processes everything locally in your browser:

  • Your files never leave your computer or mobile device
  • We cannot access, view, or store your documents
  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No tracking of your tool usage

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal Information (names, emails, phone numbers)
  • File Content (PDFs, images, or any files processed)
  • Usage Data (which tools you use or how often)
  • Browsing History
  • Device Information
  • IP Addresses

GDPR Compliance

Our architecture naturally complies with GDPR principles:

  • Data minimization (we don't collect data)
  • Purpose limitation (local processing only)
  • Storage limitation (no persistent storage)
  • Security (end-to-end encryption)
