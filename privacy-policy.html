Privacy Policy

Your privacy is a core part of how MC NovaTools is designed. This page explains our browser-first approach, what data we do not intentionally collect, and how we handle privacy-related questions.

Last Updated: January 2026

Introduction

MC NovaTools is designed to provide practical online tools with a privacy-conscious approach. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tool pages.

Our Core Privacy Approach

Many of our workflows are designed to run locally in the browser wherever possible. Our goal is to reduce unnecessary data transfer and keep common tool use simple, fast, and privacy-conscious.

  • Your usage should not require forced account creation
  • We aim to minimize unnecessary data handling
  • We do not position MC NovaTools as a file-hosting platform
  • We aim for clear and transparent site ownership and contactability

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect as Part of Tool Use

  • Personal accounts for basic access
  • Routine uploads for standard browser-based tool flows
  • Persistent user profiles for ordinary tool usage
  • Unnecessary personal information to access public utility pages

Local Processing

Where tools are implemented as browser-first utilities, processing is intended to happen on the client side. This helps reduce privacy exposure and keeps common workflows lightweight. Specific tool behavior may vary depending on implementation and future changes, so users should review the page they are using and exercise normal caution with sensitive data.

Analytics and Operational Data

We may use limited operational and analytics tooling to understand site performance, stability, or usage trends at a high level. This is not intended to turn MC NovaTools into a personal-data-heavy service, and we aim to keep data handling proportionate and minimal.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

MC NovaTools may use essential site technologies and, where applicable, analytics-related storage or similar browser mechanisms. For more detail, users should review the Cookie Policy page when available.

Third-Party Services

Some infrastructure or integrations may rely on third-party services such as hosting, deployment, analytics, or communication tools. Those services may process technical request data as part of normal web delivery.

Data Security

We aim to use a practical security posture appropriate to the nature of the site, including secure hosting practices, limited complexity where possible, and browser-first workflows for many tools. No internet service can promise absolute security, but we try to reduce unnecessary risk.

Your Questions and Contact

If you have privacy-related questions, concerns, or requests about this policy, you can contact the site owner directly.
