Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our foundation

Last Updated: January 2026

Introduction

MC NovaTools ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and online tools. Our approach is simple: we don't collect your data. All processing happens locally in your browser, ensuring complete privacy and security.

This Privacy Policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Union, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents, and other applicable privacy laws worldwide.

Our Core Privacy Principle

Unlike most online services that require you to upload files and data to their servers, MC NovaTools uses cutting-edge browser technology to process everything locally on your device. This means:

  • Your files never leave your computer or mobile device
  • We cannot access, view, or store your documents
  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No tracking of your tool usage
  • No cookies for tracking or advertising purposes

Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be absolutely clear about what we don't collect:

  • Personal Information: We don't collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses unless you voluntarily contact us
  • File Content: PDFs, images, or any files processed through our tools are never transmitted to our servers
  • Usage Data: We don't track which tools you use or how often
  • Browsing History: We don't monitor your activity on or off our site
  • Device Information: We don't collect information about your device, browser, or operating system
  • IP Addresses: We don't log or store IP addresses

Limited Information Processing

The only information that interacts with our systems is:

1. Anonymous Analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics that collect only aggregate, non-identifiable information such as:

  • Total number of visitors (not individual tracking)
  • General geographic regions (country-level only)
  • Popular tools (aggregate usage statistics)

This data cannot be used to identify individual users and is used solely for improving our services.

2. Contact Form Submissions

If you choose to contact us through our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name (as provided)
  • Email address (for response purposes only)
  • Message content

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is never shared with third parties.

How Local Processing Works

Our tools use WebAssembly and modern JavaScript to perform complex operations directly in your browser:

  1. You upload or input data into a tool
  2. The processing occurs on your device's processor
  3. Results are displayed immediately
  4. No data is transmitted to external servers

This architecture ensures maximum privacy and also means our tools work offline after initial page load.

Third-Party Services

We minimize third-party integrations. Current exceptions include:

  • Hosting Provider: Our website is hosted on secure servers. They have access only to standard server logs (IP addresses, timestamps) for security purposes
  • CDN: We may use content delivery networks to serve static assets faster

These providers are bound by strict data processing agreements and cannot use your data for their own purposes.

Your Rights Under GDPR and CCPA

Depending on your location, you have certain rights regarding your personal information:

GDPR Rights (EU Users)

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation on data processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of data to another service
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal data

CCPA Rights (California Residents)

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of collected personal information
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of sale of personal information (we never sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of privacy choices

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

Data Security

While we don't collect personal data, we take security seriously:

  • HTTPS encryption for all website connections
  • Regular security audits of our codebase
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) to prevent XSS attacks
  • No third-party scripts that could compromise privacy

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Significant changes will be announced on our website.
