How to Flip Images

  1. Upload your image by dragging and dropping or clicking to browse.
  2. Select horizontal flip to mirror left-to-right (like a mirror reflection).
  3. Select vertical flip to mirror top-to-bottom (upside down).
  4. Use "Flip Both" to apply both transformations at once.
  5. Preview updates instantly - click Download when satisfied.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between flip and rotate?

Flip creates a mirror image by reversing the image along an axis. Rotate turns the image around its center point. A horizontal flip is what you see when looking in a mirror.

Does flipping affect image quality?

No! Flipping is a lossless transformation that doesn't reduce image quality or file size. The image is simply mirrored without any interpolation or compression.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! All image processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Can I flip the image multiple times?

Yes! Clicking the same flip option again will return the image to its original orientation. You can toggle between flipped and original states as many times as you like.
