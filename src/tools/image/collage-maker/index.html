How to Create Photo Collages

  1. Upload multiple images by dragging and dropping or clicking to browse.
  2. Select your preferred grid layout from the available options (2×2, 3×3, 4×4, etc.).
  3. Adjust the gap spacing between images for your desired look.
  4. Choose a background color for your collage.
  5. Drag and drop thumbnails to reorder images if needed.
  6. Click "Create Collage" and download your creation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many images can I include in a collage?

The number of images depends on the layout you choose. 2×2 supports 4 images, 3×3 supports 9, and 4×4 supports up to 16 images. You can also use partial layouts.

Can I reorder the images in my collage?

Yes! Simply drag and drop the thumbnail images in the controls panel to reorder them. The collage preview will update automatically.

What is the maximum file size?

The total size of all uploaded images should not exceed 30MB. Individual images are automatically resized to fit the collage layout.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! All collage creation happens entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.
