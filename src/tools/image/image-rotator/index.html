  3. Use the flip buttons to mirror your image horizontally or vertically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will rotating affect image quality?

For 90°, 180°, and 270° rotations, no quality is lost as these are lossless transformations. For custom angles, there may be slight quality reduction due to interpolation.

What's the difference between flip and rotate?

Flip creates a mirror image (reverses the image along an axis), while rotate turns the entire image around its center point. Flipping horizontally is like looking at the image in a mirror.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! All image processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Can I rotate by any angle?

Yes! You can enter any angle from -360° to 360°. Positive values rotate clockwise, negative values rotate counter-clockwise.
