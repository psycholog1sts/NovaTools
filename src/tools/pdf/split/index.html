Split PDF Online - Extract Pages from PDF Free

Need to split PDF documents or extract PDF pages for sharing specific sections? Our free PDF splitter makes it easy to separate PDF files into individual pages or extract just the pages you need. Whether you want to remove unwanted pages, create separate documents from specific chapters, or extract important pages for email attachments, our browser-based tool handles it all. Simply upload your PDF, specify the page range using formats like "1-5" for a range or "1,3,5" for specific pages, and download your extracted pages as a single PDF instantly. Leave the page range empty to split every page into its own PDF, delivered as a ZIP. All processing happens client-side in your browser, ensuring your confidential documents remain private. No registration, no watermarks, and no software installation required - just fast, secure PDF splitting whenever you need it.

How to Use Our PDF Split Tool

  1. Upload your PDF: Drag and drop your PDF file into the upload area, or click to browse and select from your computer. Files up to 50MB are supported.
  2. Specify page range: Enter the pages you want to extract using formats like "1-5" for pages 1 through 5, or "1,3,5" for specific pages. Leave empty to split all pages individually.
  3. Split: Click the "Split PDF" button to process your document instantly in your browser.
  4. Download: Save your extracted pages or individual split files to your device immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract multiple separate page ranges at once?
Yes! You can specify multiple page ranges using comma-separated values. For example, entering "1-3,5,8-10" will extract pages 1 through 3, page 5, and pages 8 through 10 into a single document. Leave the field empty to split every page into individual files.
Is my PDF content secure when splitting online?
Absolutely! Our PDF split tool processes everything locally in your browser. Your document is never uploaded to any server, meaning sensitive information in your PDFs stays completely private and secure on your device throughout the entire process.
Will the extracted pages maintain the original PDF quality?
Yes, the extracted pages maintain 100% of the original PDF quality. Our tool simply separates the pages without re-encoding or compressing the content, ensuring your text remains crisp and images stay sharp in the extracted files.

What the PDF Splitter Does and Why It Matters

The PDF Splitter takes one PDF and breaks it into individual pages or extracts the specific pages and ranges you choose, all in your browser. It uses the page structure of the original to produce clean, separate output without re-rendering the content.

This matters when you only need part of a document — pulling a single signed page out of a long contract, separating a scanned batch, or sending just chapter three. Splitting locally avoids uploading the whole confidential file to an online service to grab a few pages.

How to Use PDF Splitter

  1. Upload the PDF you want to split.
  2. Choose to split every page individually, or specify pages and ranges (for example 1-3, 5).
  3. Run the split to generate the selected output.
  4. Download the resulting page(s) or the extracted range.

Supported Inputs and Limitations

What you provide

  • A single PDF file
  • A page selection: all pages, or specific pages and ranges

What you get

  • A single PDF containing the pages you selected, or — when you leave the range empty — every page as its own PDF packaged in a ZIP
  • Downloadable files containing only what you asked for

Known limitations

  • It operates on page boundaries; it cannot split a single page into pieces.
  • Encrypted PDFs may need to be unlocked first.
  • Very large documents depend on available browser memory.

Privacy and Security

Splitting is performed entirely in your browser using pdf-lib. The PDF you upload is processed on your device and is never sent to NovaTools or any external service. The pdf-lib library itself is loaded from a public CDN.
