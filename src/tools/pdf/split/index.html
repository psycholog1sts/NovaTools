Yes! You can specify multiple page ranges using comma-separated values. For example, entering "1-3,5,8-10" will extract pages 1 through 3, page 5, and pages 8 through 10 into a single document. Leave the field empty to split every page into individual files.
Is my PDF content secure when splitting online?
Absolutely! Our PDF split tool processes everything locally in your browser. Your document is never uploaded to any server, meaning sensitive information in your PDFs stays completely private and secure on your device throughout the entire process.
Will the extracted pages maintain the original PDF quality?
Yes, the extracted pages maintain 100% of the original PDF quality. Our tool simply separates the pages without re-encoding or compressing the content, ensuring your text remains crisp and images stay sharp in the extracted files.
