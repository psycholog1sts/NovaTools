About Expense Tracker

Our Expense Tracker helps you monitor your daily spending and income. By categorizing transactions and tracking your balance over time, you can better understand your financial habits and make informed decisions about your money. All data stays on your device for complete privacy.

How to Use

  1. Enter a description for your transaction (e.g., "Grocery shopping").
  2. Enter the amount.
  3. Select whether it's an Expense or Income.
  4. Choose a category that best fits the transaction.
  5. Select the date (defaults to today).
  6. Click Add to record the transaction.
  7. Monitor your balance, total income, and total expenses at the top.
  8. Use the filter dropdown to view only expenses or income.
  9. Click the X button to delete any transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my financial data stored?

All your transaction data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device or gets transmitted to any server. This ensures complete privacy but means your data is only available on this device.

Can I export my expense data?

Currently, direct export is not available. However, you can manually copy your transaction history. We recommend periodically recording important financial data in a separate secure location for backup.

What categories are available?

We provide common categories: Food & Dining, Transportation, Shopping, Entertainment, Bills & Utilities, Health & Medical, and Other. Choose the category that best describes your transaction for better tracking.

Is this tool suitable for business accounting?

This tool is designed for personal expense tracking. For business accounting, you should use dedicated accounting software that provides proper reporting, tax features, and data backup options.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Clearing your browser data will permanently delete all your transactions and balance information. Make sure to record any important financial information elsewhere before clearing browser data.
