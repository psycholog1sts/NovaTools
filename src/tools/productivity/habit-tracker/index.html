About Habit Tracker

Building good habits is key to personal growth and achieving your goals. Our Habit Tracker helps you visualize your progress, maintain streaks, and stay accountable. Whether you want to exercise daily, read more books, drink more water, or any other habit, this tool makes tracking simple and rewarding.

How to Use

  1. Enter a habit name in the input field (e.g., "Exercise for 30 minutes").
  2. Optionally choose an emoji to represent your habit.
  3. Click Add Habit to create your tracker.
  4. Each habit displays a monthly calendar view of your progress.
  5. Click the checkmark button to mark today's habit as complete.
  6. Watch your streak counter grow as you maintain consistency.
  7. Use the delete button to remove habits you no longer want to track.
  8. Monitor your overall progress with the statistics at the top.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my streak calculated?

Your streak counts consecutive days of completing a habit. If you miss a day, the streak resets to zero. The streak motivates you to maintain consistency and not break the chain.

Can I track habits from previous days?

Currently, you can only mark today's habit as complete. This encourages forming the habit of using the tracker daily. We may add backdating features in future updates.

Is there a limit to how many habits I can track?

There's no strict limit, but we recommend focusing on 3-5 habits at a time for better success rates. Too many habits can be overwhelming and harder to maintain.

Will my habit data be lost if I clear my browser?

Yes, clearing your browser data will delete all habit information. We recommend taking periodic screenshots of your progress as backup. Your data is never transmitted to our servers.

What's the best way to build a new habit?

Start small, be consistent, and track your progress. Research suggests it takes about 21-66 days to form a habit. Use this tracker daily, celebrate small wins, and don't be too hard on yourself if you miss a day—just get back on track the next day.
