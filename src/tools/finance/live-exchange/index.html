What Live Exchange Converter Does and Why It Matters

Live Exchange Converter converts between TRY, USD, and EUR using rates based on Turkish Central Bank (TCMB) reference data, with a cached fallback if the live source is temporarily unavailable, plus a short trend chart showing recent movement.

TCMB reference rates are a standard, widely-cited benchmark in Turkey, but they're not necessarily the exact rate a bank, exchange office, or card network will apply to an actual transaction — those typically add their own margin. This gives you the reference figure and recent trend quickly, which is useful for tracking and planning even though it's not the number you'll be charged at the counter.

How to Use Live Exchange Converter

  1. Select the currency pair (TRY↔USD, TRY↔EUR, or USD↔EUR).
  2. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  3. The converted amount and current reference rate are shown, along with a short recent-trend chart.

What to Know

  • Rates are based on TCMB reference data, refreshed periodically — if the live source is briefly unavailable, a recent cached rate is shown instead so the tool keeps working
  • Banks and exchange offices apply their own margin on top of the reference rate for actual transactions, so this won't exactly match what you're charged
  • The trend chart reflects recent movement, not a forecast of where the rate is headed

Privacy and Security

The amount and currencies you select are used only to calculate the result shown and aren't sent anywhere or stored. Only the exchange-rate data itself is fetched from an external source.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the exact rate my bank will give me?

No — this is the TCMB reference rate. Banks and exchange offices apply their own buy/sell margin on top, so the rate you're actually offered for a real transaction will differ somewhat.

What happens if the live rate source is unavailable?

The tool falls back to a recently cached rate rather than showing an error, so you still get a usable (if slightly less current) figure.

How often does the rate update?

Periodically, aligned with TCMB's own reference-rate publishing schedule rather than continuous real-time ticking — for anything time-sensitive, confirm the current rate with your bank or exchange provider directly.
