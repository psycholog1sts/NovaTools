What Student Loan Payoff Calculator Does and Why It Matters

Student Loan Payoff Calculator projects how long it will take to pay off a student loan given the balance, interest rate, and monthly payment, and shows how an extra payment amount changes both the payoff timeline and the total interest paid.

The effect of a modest extra payment on total interest is often bigger than people expect, because it reduces the principal balance sooner, which in turn reduces how much interest accrues on every following payment. Seeing the actual numbers — months saved and interest saved — makes that tradeoff concrete instead of a vague "extra payments help" generalization.

How to Use Student Loan Payoff Calculator

  1. Enter your current loan balance and interest rate.
  2. Enter your regular monthly payment.
  3. Optionally add an extra payment amount to see its effect.
  4. Review the projected payoff timeline, total interest, and the payment schedule.

What to Know

  • This projects a standard amortizing payoff based on the numbers you enter — it doesn't model variable-rate loans where the rate can change over the loan term
  • In Turkey, this doesn't cover KYK loan-specific terms or forgiveness rules — check directly with KYK or your bank for the exact terms of a KYK or bank student loan
  • This is a planning estimate, not a payoff quote from your loan servicer — confirm your exact current balance and rate with them before making payment decisions

Privacy and Security

The loan details you enter are used only to calculate the projection shown on this page and aren't sent anywhere or stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a small extra payment save so much interest?

Because it reduces the principal balance immediately, which lowers the interest that accrues on every subsequent payment for the rest of the loan — the earlier the extra payment, the bigger the compounding benefit over the full term.

Does this account for KYK-specific repayment rules?

No — this is a general amortization calculator. For KYK loan specifics (repayment terms, deferment, forgiveness conditions), check directly with KYK.

What if my loan has a variable interest rate?

This calculator assumes a fixed rate for the projection period. If your loan's rate can change, treat the result as an estimate based on the current rate, and recheck the projection if your rate changes.
