What Tax Estimator Does and Why It Matters

Tax Estimator calculates an estimated net salary from a gross salary figure, applying 2026 Turkish payroll deductions — income tax, SGK (social security) premiums, unemployment insurance, and stamp duty (damga vergisi) — and also shows the employer's total cost for that salary.

Gross-to-net payroll math in Turkey involves several separate deduction brackets and rates that change from year to year, which makes a manual calculation error-prone. This gives a quick, current-year estimate for salary negotiations, job offer comparisons, or budgeting — useful as a planning figure, though your actual payslip is the authoritative number.

How to Use Tax Estimator

  1. Enter the gross monthly salary figure.
  2. The estimator applies current 2026 income tax, SGK, unemployment insurance, and stamp duty deductions.
  3. Review the estimated net salary and the total employer cost.

What to Know

  • Based on 2026 Turkish payroll rates and brackets — figures will need updating in future years as rates and brackets change
  • Doesn't account for every individual circumstance (additional benefits, specific exemptions, or minimum-wage-specific tax exemptions that may apply) — actual payroll deductions can differ based on your specific situation
  • This is a planning estimate, not a payslip — your employer's payroll department or a mali müşavir (accountant) has the authoritative figure for your actual situation

Privacy and Security

The salary figure you enter is used only to calculate the estimate shown on this page and isn't sent anywhere or stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why might my actual payslip differ from this estimate?

Individual circumstances — specific exemptions, benefits, or bracket effects that build up over the tax year — can shift the exact figure. Treat this as a close planning estimate and use your actual payslip or your employer's payroll department for the authoritative number.

Does this include SGK and unemployment insurance?

Yes, both are included in the deduction estimate, along with income tax and stamp duty, giving a full gross-to-net picture rather than just the income tax portion.

Will this still be accurate next year?

Turkish payroll tax brackets and rates are typically updated annually. This estimator reflects 2026 rates — check for an updated version, or confirm current rates with a mali müşavir, for calculations in a later year.
