About Word Counter

Our free Word Counter tool provides instant text analysis with comprehensive statistics. Whether you're writing an essay, blog post, social media content, or professional document, this tool helps you track word count, character limits, reading time, and keyword density in real-time.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text into the textarea above.
  2. View real-time statistics: words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs.
  3. Check estimated reading time and speaking time.
  4. Analyze top 5 keywords for content optimization.
  5. Use Copy to copy your text to clipboard.
  6. Click Clear to reset the textarea.

Features Explained

Word Count: Total number of words separated by spaces.
Character Count: Total characters including spaces, with a separate count excluding spaces.
Sentence Count: Number of sentences determined by punctuation marks (., !, ?).
Paragraph Count: Number of text blocks separated by line breaks.
Reading Time: Estimated time to read the text at 200 words per minute.
Speaking Time: Estimated time to speak the text at 130 words per minute.
Keyword Density: Top 5 most frequently used words (excluding common stop words).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored or sent to a server?

No. All text processing happens entirely within your browser. Your text is never transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere.

How accurate is the reading time estimate?

The reading time is calculated based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute, which is typical for adults reading English text.

What are stop words in keyword density?

Stop words are common words (like "the", "and", "is", "of") that are filtered out when analyzing keyword density.

Can I use this for Twitter/X character limits?

Yes! The character count shows both total characters and characters without spaces. Twitter/X has a 280 character limit.

Does this work for all languages?

The word and character counting works for any Unicode text. However, sentence detection and keyword analysis are optimized for English.
