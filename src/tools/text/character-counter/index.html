About Character Counter

Our free Character Counter tool provides detailed text analysis with comprehensive character breakdowns. Whether you're crafting the perfect tweet, optimizing your Instagram caption, or checking content limits, this tool helps you track every character type and stay within platform limits.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text into the textarea above.
  2. View real-time statistics: total characters, letters, numbers, special characters, spaces, and lines.
  3. Check social media limit progress bars for Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube.
  4. Use Copy to copy your text to clipboard.
  5. Click Clear to reset the textarea.

Features Explained

Total Characters: All characters including spaces and line breaks.
Without Spaces: Characters excluding whitespace.
Letters: Alphabetic characters (A-Z, a-z).
Numbers: Numeric digits (0-9).
Special Characters: All other characters including punctuation and symbols.
Spaces: Space, tab, and other whitespace characters.
Lines: Number of lines in the text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored or sent to a server?

No. All text processing happens entirely within your browser. Your text is never transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere.

How do emoji count as characters?

Emojis may count as 1 or 2 characters depending on the platform. This tool counts each Unicode code point. Some platforms treat certain emojis (like flags or skin tone modifiers) as multiple characters.

What's the difference between with and without spaces?

The "without spaces" count removes all whitespace characters (spaces, tabs, line breaks) from the total. This is useful for platforms that have different character counting rules.

Why are social media limits important?

Each social platform has specific character limits for posts, captions, and descriptions. Exceeding these limits can result in truncated content or failed posts.

Can I count characters for SMS text messages?

Yes! Standard SMS messages are limited to 160 characters (GSM-7 encoding) or 70 characters (Unicode). Use the total character count and aim for under 160 characters for single SMS messages.
