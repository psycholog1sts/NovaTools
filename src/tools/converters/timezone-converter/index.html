About Time Zone Converter

Our free Time Zone Converter helps you convert time between different time zones worldwide. Whether you're scheduling international meetings, traveling across time zones, or communicating with people around the globe, this tool makes it easy to find the right time.

How to Use

  1. Select your source time zone from the "From Time Zone" dropdown.
  2. Select your target time zone from the "To Time Zone" dropdown.
  3. Enter the date and time you want to convert.
  4. Click "Convert Time" to see the converted time.
  5. View the time difference between the two zones.
  6. Use "Use Current Time" to quickly convert the current time.
  7. Click "Copy Result" to copy the converted time to your clipboard.

Supported Time Zones

North America: New York, Chicago, Denver, Los Angeles
Europe: London, Paris, Berlin, Moscow, Istanbul
Asia: Dubai, Mumbai, Shanghai, Tokyo, Seoul
Pacific: Sydney, Auckland
Universal: UTC

Frequently Asked Questions

How does daylight saving time affect conversions?

This converter automatically accounts for daylight saving time (DST) where applicable. The current time display shows whether DST is in effect for each time zone.

What is UTC?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the primary time standard by which the world regulates clocks and time. It does not observe daylight saving time and serves as a reference point for all other time zones.

Can I convert dates in the past or future?

Yes, you can enter any date and time for conversion. The tool will correctly calculate the time difference, including historical or future daylight saving time rules.

Is my data stored?

No. All conversions happen locally in your browser. No data is transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere.

Why is the time difference not a whole number of hours?

Some time zones use offsets that include minutes (e.g., India is UTC+5:30, Nepal is UTC+5:45). This converter handles all time zone offsets accurately.
