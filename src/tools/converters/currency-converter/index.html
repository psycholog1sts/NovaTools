About Currency Converter

Our free Currency Converter helps you convert between 150+ world currencies instantly. Whether you're traveling abroad, shopping online, or managing international finances, this tool provides quick and easy currency conversions with current exchange rates.

How to Use

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert in the Amount field.
  2. Select the source currency (the currency you have) from the "From" dropdown.
  3. Select the target currency (the currency you want) from the "To" dropdown.
  4. The converted amount appears instantly below.
  5. The current exchange rate is displayed for your reference.
  6. Use the Swap button to quickly reverse the conversion direction.
  7. Click Copy Result to copy the conversion to your clipboard.

Supported Currencies

Major Currencies: US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), Japanese Yen (JPY), Swiss Franc (CHF), Canadian Dollar (CAD), Australian Dollar (AUD)
Asian Currencies: Chinese Yuan (CNY), Indian Rupee (INR), Singapore Dollar (SGD), South Korean Won (KRW)
Other Popular: Turkish Lira (TRY), Brazilian Real (BRL), Mexican Peso (MXN), Russian Ruble (RUB)

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the exchange rates?

The exchange rates displayed are for informational purposes and updated periodically. They are approximate rates and may differ from the actual rates offered by banks and forex providers. For financial transactions, always confirm rates with your bank.

How often are rates updated?

Rates are typically updated once per day. For the most current market rates, please consult a financial service provider or your bank.

Is there a limit on conversion amounts?

No, you can enter any amount for conversion. The tool handles both small and large numbers accurately.

Can I see historical exchange rates?

This tool shows current exchange rates only. For historical rate data, please consult a financial data provider.

Is my conversion data stored?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser. No data is transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere.
