About Age Calculator

Our free Age Calculator helps you determine your exact age in years, months, and days from your birthdate. It also provides additional information like the day of the week you were born, your zodiac sign, and a countdown to your next birthday.

How to Use

  1. Click on the date input field to open the date picker.
  2. Select your birthdate from the calendar.
  3. Click the "Calculate Age" button to see your results.
  4. View your age broken down into years, months, and days.
  5. See additional details like day born, zodiac sign, and fun facts.
  6. Check the countdown to your next birthday!
  7. Use Clear to reset and calculate another date.

What You'll Learn

Exact Age: Your age precisely calculated in years, months, and days.
Day of Birth: The day of the week you were born (Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
Zodiac Sign: Your astrological sign based on your birth date.
Life Statistics: Fun estimates like approximate heartbeats, breaths taken, and hours slept.
Next Birthday: Countdown showing days until your next birthday.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the age calculation?

The age calculation is mathematically accurate based on the dates provided. It accounts for varying month lengths and leap years.

Does this tool store my birthdate?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your birthdate is never transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere.

How are the fun facts calculated?

The fun facts are rough estimates based on average human statistics. For example, average heart rate is about 80 beats per minute, and average sleep is about 8 hours per day.

Can I calculate age for any date?

Yes, you can enter any past date to calculate the time elapsed. However, you cannot enter future dates as the calculator is designed to determine age from a birthdate.

How is the zodiac sign determined?

The zodiac sign is determined based on standard astrological date ranges for each sign, from Aries (March 21 - April 19) to Pisces (February 19 - March 20).
