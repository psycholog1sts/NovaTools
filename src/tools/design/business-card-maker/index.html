Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard business card dimensions?

The standard US business card size is 3.5 x 2 inches (89 x 51 mm). Our tool creates cards at this standard size with proper bleed areas for professional printing.

Can I print these cards at home?

Yes! Download the PNG version for home printing on cardstock. For best results, use high-quality business card paper and ensure your printer settings match the card dimensions.

Is the PDF print-ready?

Yes, the PDF is generated at 300 DPI, which is the standard resolution for professional printing. You can send it directly to any print shop.

Can I add a logo to my business card?

Currently, this version supports text-based designs. Use our Logo Maker tool to create a logo, then combine it with your business card design in any image editor.

Is my contact information secure?

Absolutely. All data is processed locally in your browser. Your contact information is never sent to or stored on our servers.
