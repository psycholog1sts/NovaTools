About QR Code Designer

Create stunning, branded QR codes that match your visual identity. Our QR Code Designer lets you customize colors while maintaining scan reliability. Generate codes for websites, contact info, WiFi networks, plain text, and more. Perfect for marketing materials, business cards, and product packaging.

How to Use

  1. Select the QR code type (URL, Text, Email, WiFi, or Phone).
  2. Enter the data you want to encode based on the selected type.
  3. Choose your QR code color to match your brand.
  4. Set the background color (keep high contrast for best scanning).
  5. Select error correction level (higher = more robust but larger code).
  6. Preview and test the QR code with your phone.
  7. Download as PNG for digital use or SVG for print.

QR Code Type Guide

  • URL - Link to websites, landing pages, or specific web content.
  • Text - Display plain text messages when scanned.
  • Email - Open email client with pre-filled address, subject, and body.
  • WiFi - Connect to wireless networks automatically.
  • Phone - Initiate phone calls when scanned.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will custom colors affect scanning?

Custom colors can affect scanning if there's not enough contrast. Keep the QR color dark and background light. Always test your custom-colored QR codes with multiple devices and lighting conditions before publishing.

What is error correction?

Error correction allows QR codes to be readable even if partially damaged or obscured. Higher levels provide more redundancy but create denser codes. Use High (30%) for codes that will be displayed in harsh conditions or might get damaged.

Can I add a logo to my QR code?

This version focuses on color customization. For logo integration, generate a high error correction QR code and overlay your logo in the center using image editing software.

Are these QR codes permanent?

QR codes encode static data directly. Once generated, they always contain the same information. For changeable QR codes (like for marketing campaigns), consider using a URL shortener service that allows redirect updates.

Is my QR code data private?

Yes! All QR code generation happens locally in your browser. Your URLs, WiFi passwords, and other data never leave your device or touch our servers.
