About Mockup Generator

Create professional device mockups to showcase your websites, apps, and designs. Simply upload a screenshot and place it within realistic device frames including laptops, phones, tablets, and desktop monitors. Perfect for portfolios, presentations, marketing materials, and app store screenshots.

How to Use

  1. Select the device type you want (Laptop, Phone, Tablet, or Desktop).
  2. Upload your screenshot by clicking the upload area or dragging and dropping an image.
  3. Choose a background color that complements your design.
  4. Select the device frame color (Space Gray, Silver, Gold, or Black).
  5. Preview your mockup and adjust settings as needed.
  6. Download the final mockup as a high-quality PNG image.

Device Options

  • Laptop - Perfect for showcasing web designs, dashboards, and desktop applications.
  • Phone - Ideal for mobile app screenshots and responsive web designs.
  • Tablet - Great for tablet-optimized apps and digital publications.
  • Desktop - Best for full-screen applications and wide-format designs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What image format should I upload?

You can upload PNG, JPG, or any common image format. For best results, use high-resolution screenshots (at least 1920x1080 for desktop mockups).

Can I use this for commercial projects?

Yes! The mockups you create are free to use for both personal and commercial projects. Just ensure you have the rights to use the screenshots you upload.

Is my screenshot kept private?

Absolutely. All image processing happens entirely in your browser. Your screenshots are never uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Can I adjust the screenshot position within the device?

The current version automatically centers and scales your screenshot to fit the device frame. For best results, use screenshots with similar aspect ratios to the target device.

Why is my screenshot cropped or stretched?

The screenshot is scaled to fit the device screen area while maintaining aspect ratio. For best results, use screenshots that match the device's native resolution or aspect ratio.
