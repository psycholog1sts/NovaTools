About Resume Builder

Create professional, ATS-friendly resumes that help you stand out to employers. Our resume builder offers multiple templates designed to pass Applicant Tracking Systems while looking great to human recruiters. Export your resume as a PDF ready for job applications.

How to Use

  1. Select a template style: Classic, Modern, or Minimal.
  2. Fill in your personal information including name, contact details, and location.
  3. Write a compelling professional summary highlighting your key achievements.
  4. Add your work experience with job titles, companies, dates, and accomplishments.
  5. Include your education history and relevant skills.
  6. Preview your resume and download as PDF or print directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does ATS-friendly mean?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) friendly means the resume format is readable by automated systems that companies use to filter applications. Our templates use clean layouts and standard fonts that parse correctly.

Can I use this for free?

Yes! The resume builder is completely free to use. Create and download as many resumes as you need without any charges.

Is my resume data stored?

No. All your information is processed locally in your browser. Your resume data is never sent to or stored on our servers, ensuring complete privacy.

Which template should I choose?

Choose Classic for traditional industries like finance or law, Modern for tech and creative fields, and Minimal for a clean, contemporary look that works across industries.

How do I make my resume stand out?

Use action verbs, quantify achievements with numbers, tailor content to the job description, keep it concise (1-2 pages), and proofread carefully for errors.
