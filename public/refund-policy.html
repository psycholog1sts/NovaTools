Purchases and cancellations

Refund Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026.

This policy explains how refund and cancellation requests are handled for paid MC NovaTools digital services, including the one-time RLSProof Launch Verification service. Free tools do not require a purchase and therefore are not covered by this refund policy.

Launch Verification

RLSProof Launch Verification is a one-time, human-reviewed software-security verification service. The published scope includes reviewed findings, remediation guidance, and fix → re-test evidence. The public price is $149 USD for one verification engagement.

Payment activation is currently pending. Until a live checkout is enabled, no purchase can be completed through the RLSProof page and no payment is collected there.

Cancellation before work begins

If a paid order becomes available and you request cancellation before substantial review work begins, contact us promptly through the Contact page. We will review the request against the status of the engagement, the payment provider's transaction rules, and applicable consumer law.

Work already started or delivered

Because Launch Verification includes human analysis and a deliverable prepared for a specific repository or project, refund eligibility may be reduced once substantial review, remediation analysis, re-testing, or delivery has begun, to the extent permitted by applicable law. We do not use this provision to remove rights that cannot legally be waived.

Service not delivered or materially defective

If a paid service is not delivered as described, is materially defective, or cannot be completed for a reason attributable to us, contact us with the order details and a concise description of the issue. We will review the case and provide the remedy required by the applicable payment terms and consumer-protection law.

Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights, statutory withdrawal rights, or other protections that apply in your country. Where a payment provider or merchant of record grants stronger non-waivable refund or withdrawal rights, those rights apply to the transaction.

How to request a refund

Use the MC NovaTools Contact page and include the email address used for the order, the product or service name, the approximate purchase date, and the reason for the request. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, API keys, private repository contents, or other secrets.

Related policies

This Refund Policy should be read together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If a live checkout is later provided by a merchant of record, transaction-specific buyer terms displayed at checkout may also apply.
