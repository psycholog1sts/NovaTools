Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MC NovaTools collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you visit mc-novatools.com, use our browser-based utility tools, read our guides, interact with advertising, or contact us. We are a privacy-first tools website, and our product preference is to process files and inputs locally in your browser whenever technically possible. Local processing means that many file conversions, text operations, image adjustments, and developer utilities can run on your device without sending the source file to our servers.

This policy is written for users, publishers, advertising reviewers, and business contacts who need a clear explanation of our data practices. It does not promise that every browser operation is risk-free, and it does not replace the terms of third-party services that may be used for hosting, security, analytics, advertising, email delivery, or externally sourced data. If you do not agree with this policy, you should not use the site.

What Data We Collect and Why

We collect only the information needed to operate, secure, improve, and support the site. Technical data may include IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, browser type, operating system, device category, referring page, visited URL, timestamps, performance events, consent choices, and basic security logs. We use this data to deliver pages, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, measure aggregate performance, and maintain site reliability.

When you use a client-side tool, the input file or text is generally processed in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to MC NovaTools servers. Some tools may need external data, such as exchange rates, market prices, or API responses, and those requests may transmit limited technical information to the relevant provider. We avoid collecting file contents unless a future tool clearly discloses that server-side processing is required.

If you contact us, we collect the name, email address, subject, message, and any attachments or details you choose to provide. We use contact information to respond to your request, handle support, document privacy rights requests, review advertising issues, evaluate partnership inquiries, or investigate reported bugs. We ask users not to send sensitive records unless necessary and authorized.

Legal Basis for Processing

Where privacy laws require a legal basis, we rely on several bases depending on the context. We process technical logs and essential security data based on our legitimate interests in operating a safe, reliable, and abuse-resistant website. We process contact messages because it is necessary to respond to your request or take pre-contractual steps when you ask about business, advertising, or partnership opportunities.

We process analytics, advertising, and certain non-essential cookies based on consent where consent is legally required. You can withdraw that consent through our cookie banner or browser settings. We may also process limited records to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce terms, or respond to lawful requests.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Similar technologies include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, and device identifiers. MC NovaTools uses these technologies to keep the site functional, remember preferences, understand aggregate usage, and support advertising only when the applicable consent and configuration requirements are met.

Strictly Necessary technologies support core functionality such as security, consent records, page delivery, load balancing, and remembering whether you have opened cookie settings. These are needed for the site to work and generally cannot be disabled through our banner.

Performance and Analytics technologies help us understand page views, tool starts, tool completions, errors, Core Web Vitals, navigation patterns, and aggregate engagement. For example, Google Analytics may process event data and device information so we can improve page speed, discover broken workflows, and prioritize maintenance.

Functional technologies remember choices such as theme, language, recent tool settings, and interface preferences. These features make the site easier to use but are not intended to track users across unrelated websites.

Targeting and Advertising technologies may be used to display ads, measure ad performance, limit repetitive ads, prevent advertising fraud, and support personalized or contextual advertising depending on your consent and Google settings. For example, Google AdSense may use cookies or identifiers to serve and measure advertisements.

Third-Party Processors

We may use third-party processors that help deliver hosting, security, analytics, advertising, email, and operational services. These providers process data according to their own contracts and privacy terms. We choose providers for specific operational purposes and do not authorize them to use user data for unrelated purposes beyond the relevant service terms.

Google AdSense may collect or receive cookie identifiers, IP address, device information, browser details, ad interaction data, page URL, approximate location, and consent signals to serve ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and comply with advertising policies. Users can manage advertising choices through our cookie banner where available, browser settings, Google ad personalization settings, and industry opt-out tools. Rejecting advertising cookies may reduce personalization, but some contextual ads or fraud-prevention processing may still occur where permitted.

Google Analytics may collect page views, event data, browser and device information, approximate location, referral data, timestamps, and performance measurements when analytics consent is granted. We use analytics to understand aggregate usage, improve tools, identify broken pages, and monitor site performance. Users can opt out by rejecting analytics cookies in our consent banner, using browser privacy controls, installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on where supported, or blocking analytics scripts with privacy tools.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Server logs are normally retained for a limited operational period needed for security, debugging, and abuse prevention. Contact messages may be retained as long as needed to respond, maintain a support history, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or demonstrate that a privacy request was handled. Analytics data is retained according to the settings of the analytics provider and is reviewed periodically.

Consent records may be retained so we can honor your cookie choices and demonstrate compliance. If you delete cookies or use a different browser, you may need to set preferences again. We may preserve limited records longer when required by law, necessary to protect rights, or relevant to investigations of abuse, security incidents, or policy violations.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information we process. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and locate relevant records.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures may include HTTPS, limited access controls, security logging, dependency maintenance, browser-first processing, and careful handling of contact requests. No website can guarantee perfect security, and users should avoid submitting secrets, private keys, passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive documents unless a tool specifically requires and explains that processing.

Client-side processing helps reduce unnecessary transmission, but it does not eliminate every risk. Browser extensions, compromised devices, shared computers, malware, or user error can still expose data. Users should keep devices updated, review outputs before sharing, and clear local files when working on public or shared computers.

International Data Transfers

MC NovaTools is available globally, and third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from your local laws. Where required, we rely on contractual safeguards, provider compliance programs, consent, or other lawful transfer mechanisms to support international processing.

By using the site or contacting us, you understand that information may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where our providers operate. We aim to limit transfers to what is necessary for hosting, security, analytics, advertising, support, and legal compliance.
