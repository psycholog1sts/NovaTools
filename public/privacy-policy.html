Privacy Policy

Last updated: [LAST_UPDATED_DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how MC NovaTools collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you visit our website or use our browser-based tools. We write this policy for visitors in the United States, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Brazil, California, and other regions that expect clear privacy disclosures. It is intended to address key principles under the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, and comparable privacy laws. If a local law gives you additional rights, we will respect those rights where they apply to our operation.

MC NovaTools is built around a privacy-conscious workflow. Many tools are designed to process files and text in your browser whenever possible. For example, a text counter, JSON formatter, simple converter, or client-side file utility may complete its work locally without uploading your input to our servers. This design reduces unnecessary collection. It does not mean that no technical information is processed when you visit the site. Like most websites, we may process network, browser, consent, analytics, advertising, and contact information needed to operate, secure, improve, and fund the service.

Personal data we collect

We may collect contact information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, subject line, and message when you use a contact form or send email. We use that information to respond to support requests, privacy requests, business inquiries, bug reports, and abuse reports. We may collect technical information automatically, including IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, timestamps, consent choices, and basic interaction events. We may collect advertising and analytics identifiers when you consent to categories that use them or when applicable law allows limited processing.

We may also process tool inputs temporarily in your browser memory. In browser-first tools, that content remains on your device unless the tool clearly states that live data, remote processing, or an external API is required. If a tool uses a live exchange-rate source, weather source, public API, or similar service, the request may include technical network data needed to complete that request. We do not ask users to upload sensitive records unless a tool explicitly needs a file, and we encourage users to remove unnecessary personal data before using any utility.

Why we use data

We use data to provide and maintain the tools, respond to messages, protect the site from spam and abuse, measure performance, understand which pages are useful, maintain consent records, comply with legal obligations, and support advertising that helps keep tools free. We do not sell private file contents or intentionally build profiles from the contents users process in local browser tools. We do not use contact form messages for unrelated marketing unless you separately ask to receive updates.

Legal basis for processing

For GDPR and LGPD purposes, our legal bases may include performance of a service when you request a tool page, legitimate interests in securing and improving the site, consent for non-essential analytics or advertising cookies where required, compliance with legal obligations, and consent or legitimate interests for responding to voluntary messages. When we rely on legitimate interests, we consider whether the processing is reasonably expected, limited, and balanced against user rights. When consent is required, you may withdraw it through the consent management platform or browser controls.

Retention periods

Contact messages may be retained for up to twenty-four months unless a longer period is needed to resolve a dispute, comply with law, or maintain a record of a legal request. Consent records may be retained for the period necessary to demonstrate compliance and respect user choices. Server security logs may be retained for a limited period appropriate for abuse detection, debugging, and incident investigation. Analytics data may be retained according to the settings of the analytics provider. Browser-local tool inputs are generally not retained by MC NovaTools when processing occurs entirely on the user’s device.

Third-party processors

We may use third-party processors and services that help operate the website. Google AdSense may be used to display advertising and measure ad performance. Google Analytics may be used to understand aggregate site usage and improve content. A form provider may process contact submissions if the configured form endpoint is used. Hosting, CDN, security, and email providers may process technical data necessary to deliver pages and communications. These providers may process data in countries other than your own and may have their own privacy terms.

Cookies and similar technologies

Strictly necessary cookies or local storage entries support security, consent choices, language preferences, and essential site functions. Performance and analytics cookies help us understand page usage, traffic sources, and technical performance. Functional cookies remember preferences that improve usability. Targeting or advertising cookies may support Google AdSense, ad frequency management, fraud prevention, and ad measurement. Where required, non-essential categories should load only after consent. You can manage consent through the consent management platform and through browser settings.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access personal data, request correction or rectification, request erasure, restrict processing, object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, opt out of certain sharing or targeted advertising, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, limit use of sensitive personal information, and opt out of sale or sharing as defined by California law. Brazilian residents may have LGPD rights to confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing, and review of consent.

To exercise rights, email [CONTACT_EMAIL]. Please describe the request clearly and provide enough information for us to verify that you are the person connected to the request. We may ask for additional verification if needed. We will not discriminate against users for exercising privacy rights. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations, security needs, or the fact that we may not possess the data requested because a tool processed it locally in your browser.

Security measures

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect the site and the limited data we process. These may include HTTPS, security headers, access controls, dependency review, limited retention, spam controls, and careful separation between browser-local processing and network requests. No website can guarantee perfect security. Users should avoid sending secrets, payment information, government identifiers, medical records, or confidential files unless a secure and appropriate channel has been provided.

International transfers

Because website hosting, analytics, advertising, and email services may operate globally, data may be transferred to or accessed from countries with different data protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as contractual commitments, provider compliance mechanisms, or user consent. By using the site, you understand that technical processing may occur outside your country, subject to applicable law.

Children

MC NovaTools is not directed to children under the age required by applicable privacy law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us at [CONTACT_EMAIL] so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our tools, advertising configuration, analytics configuration, legal obligations, or business operations change. The last updated date will be revised when material changes are made. If changes materially affect user rights or processing, we will provide a reasonable notice on the site or through another appropriate method.

Advertising transparency and consent choices

Advertising helps support free access to MC NovaTools, but we recognize that advertising technology can involve identifiers, device signals, fraud-prevention checks, auction requests, and measurement events. For that reason, our advertising disclosures are intentionally separate from our description of browser-local tool processing. When Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may receive information such as the page URL, device and browser details, approximate location, consent signals, ad interaction data, and other information needed to select, deliver, limit, measure, and protect ads. In regions where consent is required for personalized advertising or related storage, the consent management platform should give users a meaningful choice before optional advertising cookies are used.

If you decline advertising cookies, you may still see contextual ads or limited ad requests depending on location, configuration, and legal requirements, but optional personalization should be restricted where consent is not granted. We do not ask users to click ads, we do not label ads as tool controls, and we do not use contact form messages or private file contents to target ads. If an advertisement appears misleading or interferes with the use of a tool, users can report the problem through the Contact page so we can review placement, policy settings, and user experience.

Data minimization and local processing

Our data minimization approach is practical rather than decorative. We try to avoid collecting information that is not needed for a clear purpose. When a task can be completed locally, the tool should perform the operation in the browser and avoid sending the content to our infrastructure. When a task needs current remote data, such as exchange rates, market data, weather information, or third-party verification, the page should make that dependency understandable. Users should still avoid processing highly sensitive data unless they have assessed the tool, the browser environment, and their own legal obligations.

Because browser-local processing happens on the user’s device, MC NovaTools may be unable to access, recover, correct, or delete the underlying tool input after the session ends. This is a privacy benefit, but it also means users must keep their own backups and verify their own outputs. If a privacy request concerns local-only data that was never transmitted to us, we will explain that limitation rather than pretend to possess information we do not have.
