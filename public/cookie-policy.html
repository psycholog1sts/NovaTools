Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how MC NovaTools uses cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, and similar technologies. We use the word cookies broadly to include small browser storage entries and identifiers that help the site remember preferences, measure performance, protect forms, and support advertising when consent and law allow it. The policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and the consent management platform shown on the site.

Strictly necessary cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are required for core site functions. They may store consent choices, language preferences, security signals, session integrity information, or basic interface settings. Without these technologies, the site may not remember whether you accepted or rejected optional categories, forms may be more vulnerable to spam, and some accessibility or preference features may not work as expected. Example names may include [COOKIE_NAME] or other names assigned by our consent or hosting configuration.

Performance and analytics cookies

Performance and analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use MC NovaTools. They may show which pages are visited, how users move through the site, what browsers are used, where errors occur, and whether content is useful. We use this information to improve navigation, speed, content quality, and tool reliability. Google Analytics may be used for aggregate reporting where enabled. Analytics cookies are not intended to collect private file contents or tool inputs processed locally in the browser.

Functional cookies

Functional cookies remember choices that improve the experience. They may store language, theme, region, or dismissed interface notices. These cookies are not always essential, but disabling them may make the site feel less consistent. For example, you may need to reselect preferences on future visits, or a notice may reappear because the browser no longer stores your choice.

Targeting and advertising cookies

Targeting and advertising cookies may be used by Google AdSense and related advertising systems to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, limit repetition, and support monetization that keeps tools free. Depending on consent settings and regional rules, ads may be contextual or may use additional signals. Advertising cookies can involve third-party processing, and Google may use data according to its own policies. We do not allow advertising placements to intentionally mimic tool buttons or block core functionality.

Managing consent

MC NovaTools references a consent management platform that lets users review categories and choose whether optional analytics or advertising technologies may run. You can manage consent from the cookie banner or cookie settings control where available. If you withdraw consent, optional scripts should stop loading on future page views or after preferences are updated. You can also clear cookies in your browser, use private browsing, block third-party cookies, or configure browser-level privacy protections.

Consequences of disabling cookies

Disabling strictly necessary storage may prevent consent preferences, security checks, or interface choices from working correctly. Disabling analytics will reduce our ability to understand aggregate site performance and prioritize improvements. Disabling functional cookies may require repeated preference selections. Disabling targeting or advertising cookies may reduce ad personalization or change ad measurement, but the site should remain usable. Some third-party services may still receive limited technical information needed to deliver content or comply with security obligations.

Cookie changes

Cookie names and providers may change when we update tools, analytics, consent controls, or advertising configuration. When we know a specific cookie name, we may list it directly; where names vary by provider configuration, we use placeholders such as [COOKIE_NAME] until the deployment configuration is finalized. We review this policy periodically so users can understand the categories, purposes, and choices available.

Consent management platform

The consent management platform is the primary place to review and change optional cookie choices on MC NovaTools. It may store a consent record showing which categories were accepted or rejected, when the choice was made, and which version of the notice applied. This record helps us respect user choices and demonstrate compliance. If the consent control is unavailable because of browser settings, extensions, network filtering, or script blocking, you can still manage cookies through browser settings, private browsing, or device-level privacy controls.

Third-party cookies and browser controls

Some cookies or similar technologies may be set by third-party services, including analytics or advertising providers, after the relevant consent category is enabled or where applicable law permits limited processing. Browser vendors increasingly restrict third-party cookies, and your actual experience may depend on the browser, region, privacy extensions, and device configuration. If you block third-party cookies, some advertising measurement, frequency capping, embedded content, or analytics functions may be limited. The core informational pages and browser-first tools should remain accessible, although preferences may not persist in the same way.

Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or remove analytics tools, advertising partners, consent features, localization features, or security controls. We may also update it when a provider changes cookie names, retention periods, or processing purposes. The most current version on this page controls. If the change is material, we will make reasonable efforts to surface the change through the site or consent interface so users can review their choices again.
