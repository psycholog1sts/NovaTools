About MC NovaTools

Building the future of free, privacy-first online tools

Our Story

MC NovaTools was founded by Metehan ÇETİN, LPC (Licensed Professional Counselor) with a simple but powerful mission: to provide professional-grade online tools that respect user privacy. In an era where most online services require account creation and data collection, we believe users deserve better.

What started as a small collection of utility tools has grown into a comprehensive platform serving millions of users worldwide. Our commitment to privacy-first architecture means all processing happens in your browser - your files and data never leave your device.

Our Mission

We believe that powerful tools should be accessible to everyone, regardless of technical expertise or financial resources. Our mission is threefold:

  • Privacy First: Zero data collection, zero tracking, zero compromises on privacy
  • Universal Access: Free tools that work for everyone, everywhere
  • Professional Quality: Enterprise-grade functionality without the enterprise price tag

What Makes Us Different

Client-Side Processing

Unlike most online tools that upload your files to remote servers, we use cutting-edge WebAssembly technology to process everything in your browser. This means:

  • Your sensitive documents never leave your device
  • Faster processing without upload/download delays
  • Works offline after initial page load
  • Zero server costs = truly free service

No Registration Required

We don't ask for your email, phone number, or social media accounts. Just open a tool and start using it. Your time is valuable - we won't waste it with unnecessary signup processes.
